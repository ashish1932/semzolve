Methoxypropyl Acetate

Synonyms: PMA | PGMEA | Propylene Glycol Methyl Ether Acetate | 1-Methoxy-2-propyl acetate

High-purity organic ester solvent characterized by a mild pleasant odor, excellent solvency for acrylics, epoxies, and polyurethanes, and a moderate evaporation rate. Critical feedstock in semiconductor photoresists and automotive finishes.

Boiling Point: 146 °C
Flash Point: 45 °C
Density: 0.966 g/cm³
Moisture: < 0.05%
Appearance: Clear Liquid
