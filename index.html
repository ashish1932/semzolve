HOW IT WORKS

Trade-to-Cash: Four Steps from Molecule to Port Delivery

Powered by the One Radar transaction engine — real-time customs intelligence, toll synthesis, and automated trade compliance.

01

RFQ & Price Discovery

Comtrade Unit Rates + Real-time FX

Instant matching against 279+ catalog molecules or custom synthesis routes. Live spot USD/INR benchmarked against Nhava Sheva and Mundra customs clearances.

02

Lab QC & Toll Synthesis

COA Verification & Controlled Batching

Pilot synthesis in our Ankleshwar & Tarapur plants. Multi-point analytical testing via HPLC, GC-MS, and ICP-OES with guaranteed certificate of analysis.

03

Automated Documentation

Proforma, Incoterms CIF/FOB, LC Check

Instant export compliance paperwork: 16-section GHS SDS, EU REACH documentation, Letter of Credit compliance audit, and automated DG declarations.

04

Cold-Chain Port Dispatch

Vessel Tracking & Customs Release

Dangerous-goods approved packaging (UN certified drums & ISO tanks). Real-time satellite vessel tracking from Indian export gates to 15+ destination ports.
