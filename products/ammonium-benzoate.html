Technical & Commercial Specifications: Ammonium Benzoate

How is Ammonium Benzoate tested and certified before dispatch?

Every production campaign of Ammonium Benzoate (CAS 1863-63-4) undergoes multi-point analytical validation in accredited laboratories before dispatch. Quality checks include minimum assay (typically ≥ 99.0% by GC-FID or HPLC), moisture determination by Karl Fischer titration (≤ 0.05%), density, refractive index, and specific impurity screening. Consignments are accompanied by an authenticated, batch-traceable Certificate of Analysis (COA) corresponding to your technical specifications.

What packaging and container loadings are available for export?

We supply Ammonium Benzoate in UN-certified industrial packaging configured for secure ocean and intermodal transport:

  • 200L Tight-head Steel or HDPE Drums: 80 drums per 20ft FCL (16.0 MT net), palletized and strapped with moisture-barrier stretch wrap.
  • 1,000L Composite Intermediate Bulk Containers (IBC): 18 to 20 units per 20ft FCL (18.0 to 20.0 MT net).
  • 20–24 MT Bulk ISO Tank Containers: Insulated, dedicated tanks for bulk industrial users.
Analytical evaluation samples (500g to 5kg) can be air-freighted under appropriate IATA Dangerous Goods protocols upon request.

What export documentation and compliance certificates are included?

Through our One Radar trade operations system, all export shipments from One Chemicals and Semzolve are issued with clean, audit-ready regulatory dossiers:

  • Batch Certificate of Analysis (COA) and Technical Data Sheet (TDS).
  • GHS-compliant 16-section Safety Data Sheet (SDS) formatted to destination country regulations (EU CLP / US OSHA).
  • Non-Preferential Certificate of Origin (COO), Commercial Tax Invoice under GST LUT zero-rated export status, and detailed Packing List.
  • Bill of Lading (B/L) issued by tier-1 shipping lines with real-time container tracking.
Our partner manufacturing facilities operate under ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015, and cGMP quality frameworks with EU REACH registration support.

What are the typical lead times and shipping routes?

For standard catalogue grades, dispatch from our domestic buffer inventory at audited manufacturing hubs takes 48 to 72 hours. For custom synthesis campaigns or dedicated FCL container batches, lead time is typically 2 to 3 weeks to container stuffing. Ocean consignments load via Nhava Sheva (JNPT) or Mundra ports, with direct sailings to Hamburg, Rotterdam, Antwerp, Houston, Busan, Singapore, and Jebel Ali.

Can you tailor synthesis specifications or manufacture under an NDA?

Yes. If your process requires tightened impurity profiles, specific isomer ratios, low-water solvent grades, or custom intermediate synthesis, our parent and partner reaction hubs (500L to 10kL glass-lined and SS reactors) can accommodate campaign synthesis. We execute bilateral Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) upfront to safeguard your proprietary synthetic pathways and formulation IP.
