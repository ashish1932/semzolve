Using this catalogue

Does Semzolve manufacture all of these products?

We operate through an integrated synthesis and partner corridor. Semzolve and our parent manufacturing infrastructure produce the pharma intermediates, specialty solvents, glycol esters, and cosmetic active ingredients. Petrochemicals, base solvents, and commodity industrial acids are imported, stocked, and distributed from audited bulk terminals. Each product detail page specifies synthesis pathway, assay purity, and technical compliance.

How do I find a product by CAS number?

Type the CAS number (e.g. 141-78-6 or 504-02-9) directly into the search bar above. The table filters in real-time as you type.

What packaging formats and lead times are available?

We dispatch products in ISO Tanks (20–25 MT), HDPE/Steel drums (200 kg / 250 kg), IBC totes (1,000 kg), and 25 kg multi-wall UN-approved craft bags. Domestic dispatch takes 2–4 days, and ocean freight departs regularly from Nhava Sheva (JNPT), Mundra, and Hazira ports.

Can I request custom specifications or contract synthesis?

Yes. Our synthesis network supports reaction trains from 500L to 10,000L with fractional distillation, cryogenic reactions (-78°C), and high-pressure hydrogenation under strict bilateral NDAs.
