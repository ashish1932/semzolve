1. Scope & Commitment to Confidentiality

Semzolve Technologies Pvt. Ltd. and Semzolve Pvt. Ltd. ("we", "us", or "our") operate a global chemical sourcing, custom synthesis coordination, and trade facilitation platform. We are committed to protecting the commercial confidentiality, proprietary intellectual property, and personal data of our customers, partner manufacturers, and website visitors.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, process, store, and safeguard technical enquiries, chemical purchase specifications, bilateral non-disclosure documents, and corporate contact details in compliance with the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP 2023) and the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR).

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to prepare commercial chemical quotations, execute synthesis contracts, validate export documentation, and maintain regulatory traceability:

  • Corporate & Contact Identifiers: Name, professional title, corporate affiliation, verified business email, contact telephone/WhatsApp, and registered company address.
  • Chemical & Project Specifications: Target molecule names, CAS Registry Numbers, required assay percentages, impurity tolerances, required packaging (e.g. ISO tanks, UN drums, IBC totes), destination port, and delivery Incoterms.
  • Custom Synthesis Technical Data: Synthetic pathways, reaction parameters, analytical testing protocols (HPLC/GC-MS), and bilateral non-disclosure agreement (NDA) documentation.
  • Transactional & Regulatory Data: Bill of Lading details, shipping consignee instructions, Certificate of Origin (COO) applications, and GST LUT export declarations.

3. Synthetic IP & Non-Disclosure Safeguards

Strict Technical Non-Disclosure (NDA) Protocol

Chemical recipes, process routes, stereoselective steps, and bespoke intermediate inquiries shared with Semzolve are protected by bilateral NDAs signed upfront before reaction matching. Formulation data is never published, monetized, or shared with unauthorized third-party brokers.

When matching custom manufacturing campaigns to partner synthesis hubs across our verified reaction network, technical parameters are anonymized and released exclusively under signed back-to-back secrecy covenants with audited plant heads.

4. Legal Basis & Data Processing Purposes

We process your data strictly on legitimate commercial grounds:

  • Contractual Performance: Generating quotations, dispatching Certificate of Analysis (COA) batches, customs clearing, and coordinating ocean freight via Nhava Sheva (JNPT) and Mundra ports.
  • Regulatory Compliance: Fulfilling DGFT, Customs ICEGATE, SCOMET dual-use declarations, and EU REACH Only Representative (OR) recordkeeping.
  • Customer Support & Technical Consultation: Answering analytical queries, providing Safety Data Sheets (SDS), and maintaining dedicated account relationships.

5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your contact or enquiry information. Disclosures occur solely in the operational course of chemical trade:

  • Tier-1 Ocean Carriers & Customs Brokers: To book container space, generate Bills of Lading, and secure port customs entry.
  • Accredited Testing Laboratories: To perform independent pre-dispatch purity, moisture, and assay screening against batch specifications.
  • Statutory & Regulatory Authorities: When mandated by export licensing bodies, DGFT, CBIC, or international chemical regulatory agencies.

6. Data Security, Retention & Rights

All digital communications and inquiry histories are encrypted in transit via TLS 1.3 and stored on hardened servers with role-based access controls. Chemical batch records and analytical COA archives are retained for statutory compliance periods (typically 5 to 7 years) to support regulatory auditability.

Under applicable data protection laws, you retain the right to access, rectify, or request the deletion of your personal data, as well as to withdraw consent for direct communications at any time.

1. Contract Formation & Quotation Validity

These Terms of Trade govern all commercial quotations, proforma invoices, sales contracts, and export consignments issued by Semzolve Pvt. Ltd. and Semzolve Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("Seller") to international and domestic buyers ("Buyer").

Formal quotations issued by the Seller are valid for 14 calendar days from the date of issuance unless explicitly stated otherwise, subject to written acceptance and confirmation of raw material pricing and vessel space availability.

2. Delivery & Incoterms 2020 Definitions

All international consignments are quoted and executed in accordance with ICC Incoterms 2020 rules:

Incoterm Point of Origin / Port Seller Responsibility Buyer Responsibility
FOB (Free on Board) Nhava Sheva (JNPT) / Mundra, India Export clearance, port terminal handling, loading onto nominated vessel. Ocean freight booking, marine cargo insurance, import customs and destination clearance.
CFR (Cost and Freight) Named Overseas Destination Port Export clearance, ocean freight carriage to destination discharge port. Marine insurance during sea transit, destination port handling, import duties and inland haulage.

DAP (Delivered at Place) Buyer’s Named Facility / Warehouse Full door-to-door transportation to buyer's facility boundary, container tracking. Import customs clearance formalities, payment of destination VAT/import duties, and unloading.

3. Purity Benchmarks & Analytical Verification

All chemicals supplied are guaranteed to meet or exceed the analytical specifications outlined in the approved Technical Data Sheet (TDS) and batch Certificate of Analysis (COA).

  • Pre-Dispatch Analytical Checks: Prior to port dispatch, batch samples undergo assay determination (GC-FID or HPLC), Karl Fischer moisture titration (≤ 0.05% for anhydrous solvents), specific gravity, color index, and residual solvent profiling.
  • Inspection Window: The Buyer has 15 calendar days following vessel discharge at the destination port to inspect the cargo and report any analytical non-conformity.
  • Joint Independent Survey: In the event of a quality dispute, an independent internationally accredited surveyor (such as SGS, Intertek, or Bureau Veritas) mutually agreed upon shall draw composite samples for referee testing. The referee laboratory findings shall be final and binding.

4. UN Packaging & Dangerous Goods Protocols

Shipments comply strictly with the International Maritime Dangerous Goods (IMDG) Code, ICAO/IATA regulations, and port authority guidelines:

  • Tight-Head Steel / HDPE Drums: UN-certified 200L / 210L epoxy-lined containers palletized on heat-treated ISPM-15 wooden or plastic pallets (80 drums / 16 MT per 20ft FCL).
  • Composite IBC Totes: 1,000L UN-approved intermediate bulk containers with anti-static discharge fittings (18–20 units / 18–20 MT per 20ft FCL).
  • ISO Tank Containers: 20–24 MT dedicated stainless steel 316 / Hastelloy intermodal tanks with steam-heating coils and safety relief valves.

5. Commercial Payment Terms

Payment terms are established based on order volume, buyer creditworthiness, and contract structure:

  • Irrevocable Letter of Credit (LC): 100% at sight issued or confirmed by a Tier-1 international bank.
  • Cash Against Documents (CAD): Bank-to-bank collection via nominated international financial institutions against presentation of full set of clean on-board ocean Bills of Lading, COA, COO, and commercial invoice.
  • Telegraphic Transfer (T/T Wire): Structured advance payment with balance upon presentation of non-negotiable shipping copies.

6. Title, Risk of Loss & Force Majeure

Risk of loss or damage transfers to the Buyer strictly in accordance with the specified Incoterms 2020. Title to the goods remains with the Seller until full payment of the invoice amount is received and cleared.

Neither party shall be liable for failure or delay in performance caused by Force Majeure events beyond reasonable control, including acts of God, war, blockades, embargoes, sovereign sanctions, maritime piracy, major industrial explosions, or catastrophic raw material supply shutdowns.

1. Global Quality & Environmental Accreditations

At Semzolve and Semzolve Technologies, regulatory rigor is the foundation of our supply architecture. We operate and partner with facilities adhering to internationally recognized ISO quality, environmental, and occupational safety frameworks:

  • ISO 9001:2015 (Quality Management): Formalized operational procedures, batch consistency auditing, calibrated analytical instrumentation, and full lot-traceability from raw materials to final vessel loading.
  • ISO 14001:2015 (Environmental Management): Responsible solvent management, closed-loop volatile organic compound (VOC) vapor recovery, and verified zero-liquid-discharge (ZLD) effluent treatment.
  • ISO 45001:2018 (Occupational Health & Safety): Comprehensive hazchem process safety, personal protective equipment (PPE) enforcement, and emergency hazard containment systems.

2. European Union REACH Compliance

We actively support chemical importers across the European Economic Area (EEA) in meeting their obligations under Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006 (EU REACH):

  • Only Representative (OR) Coverage: European buyers can import designated catalogue solvents and intermediates under our appointed Only Representative agreements, relieving EU customers of standalone registration burdens.
  • Registration Dossier Support: Full toxicological data summaries, chemical safety reports (CSR), and tonnage tracking for custom synthesis molecules exceeding 1 MT per annum.
  • SVHC Screening: Rigorous monitoring to guarantee that supplied consignments contain zero Substances of Very High Concern (SVHC) on the ECHA Candidate List above 0.1% w/w.

3. cGMP & Pharmacopoeial Standards

For active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs), excipients, and advanced pharma building blocks, production is executed under Current Good Manufacturing Practice (cGMP) aligned with ICH Q7 guidelines:

  • Segregated cleanroom finishing suites meeting ISO Class 7 and Class 8 cleanroom particulate specifications.
  • Testing executed against monographs in the United States Pharmacopeia (USP), European Pharmacopoeia (EP), and Indian Pharmacopoeia (IP).
  • Validated analytical methods with complete batch documentation, change-control management, and stability study profiles.

4. SCOMET, DGFT & Dual-Use Export Clearances

Semzolve adheres strictly to national and international non-proliferation treaties, the Chemical Weapons Convention (CWC), and the Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT) SCOMET (Special Chemicals, Organisms, Materials, Equipment and Technologies) regime:

  • Dual-Use SCOMET Licensing: Regulated precursor intermediates are exported strictly under authenticated DGFT export authorizations supported by authenticated End-User Certificates (EUC).
  • Prohibited Materials: We maintain a strict corporate prohibition against handling, formulating, or supplying chemical-weapon agents, Schedule 1 CWC precursors, explosive compounds, or illicit narcotics.
  • Know Your Customer (KYC): Upfront verification of overseas buyers, end-use declarations, and physical consignment routing.

5. GHS Classification & 16-Section Safety Data Sheets

Every consignment is accompanied by a destination-compliant 16-section Safety Data Sheet (SDS) authored under the United Nations Globally Harmonized System of Classification and Labelling of Chemicals (GHS Rev. 8):

  • Harmonized European CLP Regulation (EC No 1272/2008) formatting with EU hazard and precautionary statements.
  • US OSHA Hazard Communication Standard (29 CFR 1910.1200) compliance.
  • Bilingual hazard labels displaying UN numbers, Proper Shipping Names (PSN), packing groups, and emergency spill response phone numbers.

6. Sustainability & Responsible Sourcing

Our manufacturing partners implement continuous sustainability optimizations including high-efficiency vacuum fractional distillation for solvent recovery, energy-efficient catalytic cascades, and biomass-fueled co-generation boilers to reduce Scope 1 and Scope 2 carbon footprints across Indian industrial corridors.

1. Synthetic Intellectual Property & Formulation Secrecy

In specialty chemicals and advanced intermediate synthesis, formula protection and process know-how are paramount. Semzolve Technologies guarantees multi-layered technical IP safeguards for every client project:

Bilateral Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs)

We execute legally binding bilateral NDAs prior to reviewing proprietary synthetic routes, reaction schemes, isomer requirements, or novel catalog requests. All formulation data is held in strict confidence.

  • Segregated Campaign Execution: Contract synthesis campaigns run under isolated batch protocols with dedicated reactor cleans and full line-clearance signoffs.
  • Restricted Synthesis Routing: Proprietary molecular schemes are disclosed exclusively to verified reaction directors and lead synthesis engineers on a need-to-know basis.
  • No Data Brokering: We do not operate open broker bazaars. Your production volumes, target prices, and formulation inquiries remain completely confidential.

2. Digital Trade Operations & One Radar Governance

Our digital trade operations platform, One Radar, centralizes RFQs, batch tracking, analytical COAs, and customs shipping documents under rigorous enterprise cybersecurity controls:

  • End-to-End Encryption: All client communications, RFQ submissions, and trade dossiers are encrypted in transit via TLS 1.3 and stored with AES-256 encryption at rest.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Multi-tenant isolation ensures buyers, manufacturers, and logistics handlers can access only their authorized consignment data.
  • Audit Logging & Immutability: Comprehensive chronological audit logging of specification approvals, analytical test results, and dispatch signoffs.

3. Tamper-Evident Physical Packaging & Supply Chain Integrity

Physical integrity is maintained from the reaction hub to overseas port discharge through certified tamper-evident mechanisms:

  • Numbered High-Security Bolt Seals: Container doors are sealed with ISO 17712 certified bolt seals immediately following stuffing, with unique serial numbers matched across the Bill of Lading and packing list.
  • Drum & IBC Tamper Seals: 200L drums feature crimped tamper-evident bung caps and pallet moisture shrink-wrapping. IBC totes are fitted with numbered valve locks.
  • Verified Transport Escort: Bulk ISO tankers travel under continuous GPS geofencing with automated route deviation alerts between reaction hubs and Nhava Sheva (JNPT) or Mundra ports.

4. Vendor Vetting & Facility Audits

Every synthesis partner across our 25+ plant network is subjected to technical, safety, and operational audits conducted by chemical engineers:

  • Physical inspection of reactor metallurgy (glass-lined, SS316, Hastelloy), pressure relief systems, and scrubbing towers.
  • Verification of raw material provenance, statutory environmental clearances (Pollution Control Board CTE/CTO), and worker safety records.
  • Regular calibration checks on QC analytical instrumentation (GC, HPLC, Karl Fischer, ICP-OES).
